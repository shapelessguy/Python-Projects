# Movies

This is the film library — the main folder of finished films (`{{output}}`), the same one Plex shows as **Movies**.

- Pick a film on the left to play it here, with any of its audio and subtitle tracks.
- Search with the box at the top. Folders are followed: what is added outside CyanHouse shows up on its own.
- Films arrive here ready: they are prepared in a staging area first and then moved in.
{{#publish}}- You can drag a film onto another tab to move it there; Plex is told automatically.
{{/publish}}
